The premise

Right educator. Right student. Right time.

Four 3rd-grade teachers — Ms. Rivera, Mr. Chen, Ms. Okafor, Dr. Patel — teach the same subject at the same time, which is exactly what lets them regroup students across the team instead of staying locked in one classroom.

Each day starts in homerooms — every dot keeps the color of the homeroom it started in, all game long, so you can watch the mixing happen. Once instruction begins, the team splits into four flexible groups by instructional need: Intensive Support, Core/On-Level, Strategic Practice, Extension. A colored ring shows how well each student is being reached right now — green, amber, or gray.

Midway through the day, the team holds Collaborative Planning Time: you'll look at real data on three specific students and decide where each of them — and each group's focus — should go for the afternoon.

Click any student during a block to see why they're colored that way, and move them yourself.

Three days, then a plain recap of how it went.
