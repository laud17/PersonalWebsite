NEXT EDUCATION WORKFORCE 101Introduction · 0 of 6
An interactive introduction for future educators

What if we reimagined the teaching profession?

Discover how educator teams can share responsibility for students, draw on different expertise, and create new possibilities for teaching and learning.

~60 minutes · Self-paced · AI feedback · Choose your school pathway

What you'll explore

You'll start with your own image of teaching, learn what the Next Education Workforce (NEW) is, choose an elementary or secondary school example, observe real educators, and make a team-based instructional decision.

AI learning coach: Your written thinking receives personalized feedback through the course's Netlify AI endpoint. You can revise your responses. There are no trick questions, and you do not have to agree with every aspect of team-based staffing.

Avoid including student names or other private information. Your responses stay in this browser until you export them; AI coaching sends your response to the configured course endpoint.

Page 2 · 10 minutes · Meet NEW

What is the Next Education Workforce?

Schools have traditionally organized teaching around one teacher responsible for one classroom. NEW explores different ways to organize educators so they can share a roster of students, collaborate, and use their collective expertise.
