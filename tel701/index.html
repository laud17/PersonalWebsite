Sequence after each run

  1. Have students inspect their jamovi output.
  2. Show the individual observations.
  3. Ask students to explain the estimated difference.
  4. Reveal the confidence interval and locate zero (for t-tests).
  5. Reveal the p-value and compare.
  6. Translate the result into educational language.

Important distinction

All data are fictional. Calculations are based on the embedded 10-student CSV. The independent-samples test uses pooled-variance Student's t; the one-way ANOVA uses the standard equal-variance F-test. The t-test 95% CIs are intervals for mean differences. ANOVA charts show separate 95% CIs for individual group means, not simultaneous intervals or post-hoc difference intervals. With such a small sample, assumptions and generalizability deserve particular caution.
